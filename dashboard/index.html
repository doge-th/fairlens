How to read this

Threshold — the model approves when its estimated default probability P(bad) is below your value. Lower = stricter.

Gap — difference in approval rates between the two age groups. 0 = approval parity.

Cost — blocking a good applicant costs 1; approving one who defaults costs 5 (the ratio in the dataset documentation). Accuracy treats both mistakes as equal — which is exactly why the default policy looks "best" there and worst on the map.
